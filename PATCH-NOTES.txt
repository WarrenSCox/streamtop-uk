WozzaWatch v6.0.0

WOZZAREAD CR RELEASE
- WozzaRead now mirrors the compact WozzaWatch chart sizing.
- Replaces large Books/Audiobooks tabs with WozzaWatch-style two-icon segmented control.
- Book icon selects Books; headphones icon selects Audiobooks.
- Swipe left/right on the WozzaRead chart switches Books <-> Audiobooks.
- Double-tap the chart switches Books <-> Audiobooks.
- Narrator names removed from audiobook rows; author remains.
- Audible cover lookup now prefers the individual Audible product page metadata to avoid promotional/composite chart artwork.
- Existing verified Top 10 fail-safe remains intact.
- Release renamed to v6.0.0.
